Why one person beats five.

Most projects pass through three or four hands before they become a real thing. A strategist tells a brand designer. The brand designer sends files to a factory. Each handoff loses something. The product on the shelf isn't the idea you started with.

I do all of it. Strategy, brand, visuals, factories. The idea you bring me is the idea that ships.

Plenty of people can make a product. Fewer can keep the original idea intact while doing it.

Based in Boston · Factories in Italy, China, India, Pakistan, US
