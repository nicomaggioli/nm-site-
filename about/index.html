What I’m focused on

From the first sketch
to the final drop.

Recently, my focus has been helping small businesses create their own footwear. I work closely with you to design the shoe, refine the details, and bring it to life.

With more than ten years working with overseas manufacturers, I’ve built relationships with trusted factories and learned what makes a well-made product. I handle the factory communication, sampling, and production, with close attention to materials, construction, and finish.

And it doesn’t stop at the shoe. I can photograph the product, create 3D visuals, build your website, and develop the advertising campaign for your drop. The product and the way you launch it should feel like one idea.

One idea. Every detail.
